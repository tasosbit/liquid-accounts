# Troubleshooting

Solutions to issues you may run into while using xChain EVM.

## Mobile

**We recommend using your wallet's built-in browser for the best mobile experience.** Most major mobile wallets include an integrated browser - open the app from there.

Things you might struggle with on regular mobile navigation:

### Transactions aren't showing up in my wallet

If you tap **Review** on mobile and the signing prompt never appears in your wallet, the connection between the app and the wallet is likely being dropped by the mobile OS or browser when switching to the background.

**Try the wallet's built-in browser instead.** It keeps the app and wallet within the same context - signing prompts arrive immediately and don't get lost when switching contexts.

### Signing isn't working reliably

If you tap **Confirm** or **Sign** in your wallet and are not seeing the transaction reflected on the network, the connection between the app and the wallet is likely being dropped after signing.

**Try the wallet's built-in browser instead.**
